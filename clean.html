FAQs

Common questions

What floor types can CleanBot handle?
CleanBot is designed for all common commercial floors used in lobbies, corridors and shared areas.
Can it clean while people are around?
Yes. CleanBot operates safely in mixed-traffic environments and avoids obstacles.
How is cleaning scheduled?
Cleaning is planned around your low-traffic windows, with zones and priorities configured during deployment.
Do we get reporting?
Yes. You receive clear reporting on cleaned zones, timings and completion status.
How long does deployment take?
Timing depends on building size and zone complexity. TritBot provides a clear plan after the site survey.
Do we need staff to operate it daily?
No. CleanBot is designed for simple operation, with TritBot handling monitoring and support.
Can CleanBot scale across multiple sites?
Yes. Routines and reporting can be standardised across portfolios.
What happens if the building layout changes?
TritBot updates mapping and schedules to keep performance consistent.
