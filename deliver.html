Safety, Security & Integration

Built for enterprise environments

TritBot Deliver is designed for environments where safety, privacy, and access control are critical. It integrates with lift systems and access rules to move safely between floors, and can use PIN-controlled handover for secure delivery. Every task is logged with time, location and completion status to support auditing and operational reporting.

FAQs

Common questions

What types of items can TritBot Deliver deliver?
Deliver is used for common internal deliveries such as amenities, linen, parcels and supplies, depending on your operating policies.
Can TritBot Deliver use lifts and access-controlled doors?
Yes. TritBot Deliver integrates with lifts and access systems as part of deployment, enabling secure floor-to-floor delivery.
How does secure handover work?
Deliveries can be configured with PIN or controlled handover, with full logging for audit trails.
Is TritBot Deliver safe around guests, residents and staff?
Yes. TritBot Deliver uses safety-first navigation and obstacle avoidance to operate in busy environments.
How quickly can we deploy?
Deployment timing depends on building size and integration requirements. TritBot completes a site survey and provides a clear rollout plan.
Do we need dedicated staff to run it?
No. Deliver is designed to reduce staff load, with TritBot handling setup, monitoring and support.
What reporting is available?
You receive delivery logs and operational reporting to support performance reviews and process improvements.
Can it scale across multiple sites?
Yes. Deliver is designed for multi-site rollouts with consistent standards and central oversight.
